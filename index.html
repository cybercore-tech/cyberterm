the render path

Every pixel has a job.

The terminal is the product. Cyberterm keeps the stack close to the metal so your commands, glyphs, and colors arrive with less ceremony.

01

PTY

Launch a real process boundary and let your shell behave like it does everywhere else. alacritty_terminal handles the protocol.

02

GLYPHS

Text is laid out and rendered as text, not a screenshot of a terminal. Glyph shaping lives in glyphon, cosmic-text, and etagere.

03

COMPOSITE

Opacity, selection, and cell backgrounds are part of the render pass. A quiet surface for loud work.

boot sequence

Put a real terminal on the desk.

One command installs the latest release into ~/.local/bin. Open a shell, point it at your work, and keep moving.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cybercore-tech/cyberterm/main/install.sh | sh
Linux x86_64release binariesMIT licensed
Alpha surface: paste is not wired yet; tabs and split panes are not implemented; the current build is one window with one PTY session. The core loop is real and the edges are being built in public.
